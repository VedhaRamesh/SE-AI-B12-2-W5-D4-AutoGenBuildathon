# Returns and Refund Policy

## Standard Return Window
* **30-Day Return Window**: We offer a **30-day money-back guarantee** starting from the confirmed date of delivery.
* **Eligible Conditions**: Items must be unused, unworn, unwashed, and returned in their original packaging with all tags, accessories, and manuals intact. Footwear and clothing must have original tags attached.

## How to Get a Refund (Step-by-Step)
1. **Visit the Returns Portal**: Navigate to `ourstore.com/returns` or click "Start a Return" under your Order History in your account dashboard.
2. **Select Items & Reason**: Enter your Order ID (e.g., ORD-4512) and postal code. Select the item you want to return (e.g., shoes, apparel) and select the return reason.
3. **Print Prepaid Shipping Label**: A complimentary prepaid shipping return label will be generated instantly for download and emailed to you.
4. **Package and Ship**: Repack the item securely in its original packaging, affix the prepaid label over the old barcode, and drop it off at any authorized courier location (UPS or FedEx drop box).
5. **Inspection & Processing**: Once our warehouse receives the returned package, our quality assurance team inspects the item within **3 to 5 business days**.
6. **Refund Disbursement**: Upon approval, the full refund is automatically credited back to your original payment method within **5 to 7 business days** (depending on your bank's processing schedule).

## Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items
* If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect product, you are entitled to a full refund or free replacement without paying any return shipping fees.
* Claims must be submitted within **7 calendar days** of package delivery.

## Non-Refundable Items
* Final sale items, personalized/engraved products, digital downloads, and electronic gift cards cannot be returned or refunded.

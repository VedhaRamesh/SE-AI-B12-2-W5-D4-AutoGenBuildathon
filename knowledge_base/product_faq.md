# Product FAQ & Replacement Policies

## Broken, Damaged, or Defective Products (e.g., Kitchen Appliances & Blenders)
* **Immediate Replacement Guarantee**: If your product (such as a kitchen blender, cookware, or electronic device) arrives broken, cracked, or malfunctioning, we provide an **immediate free replacement**.
* **7-Day Reporting Window**: Defective or broken items must be reported within **7 calendar days** of delivery.
* **Replacement Process**:
  1. Take 2 to 3 clear photos or a short video showing the broken unit, serial number label, and shipping box condition.
  2. Visit your account "Orders" page, select the broken item, and click **"Request Replacement"** (or reply to your order confirmation email attaching the photos).
  3. Our team issues a replacement order within 24 hours with complimentary express shipping.
* **Safe Disposal**: To protect customer and carrier safety, broken glass or sharp components (e.g., broken blender jars or blades) **do NOT need to be shipped back**. Please dispose of them safely after receiving replacement confirmation.

## Product Warranties
* All small kitchen appliances and electronic items come with a **1-Year Manufacturer Limited Warranty** covering motor defects, heating element issues, and electronic failures occurring under normal domestic household use.

## Cleaning and Maintenance
* Blender jars and blades are top-rack dishwasher safe. Motor bases should be wiped down with a damp cloth and never submerged in water.

# Account Management & Login Help

## Password Reset Instructions
If you forgot your password or cannot log in to your account, follow these steps to regain access:
1. **Navigate to Login**: Go to the login screen and click the **"Forgot Password?"** link located directly below the password input field.
2. **Enter Registered Email**: Enter the email address associated with your store account and click **"Send Reset Link"**.
3. **Check Your Inbox**: An automated password reset email will arrive within 2 to 3 minutes from `auth@ourstore.com`. (If you do not see it, please check your Spam, Junk, or Promotions folder).
4. **Click Secure Link**: Click the reset link inside the email. For security purposes, this password reset link is valid for **15 minutes**.
5. **Set New Password**: Create a new strong password containing at least 8 characters, including at least one uppercase letter, one number, and one special character.
6. **Log In**: Return to the login page and sign in with your updated credentials.

## Account Lockout & Security
* **Multiple Failed Attempts**: After 5 consecutive failed login attempts, your account is temporarily locked for **30 minutes** as a protective security measure against brute-force attacks.
* **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**: If you enabled 2FA, enter the 6-digit one-time code sent to your authenticator app or SMS. If you lost access to your 2FA device, utilize your emergency backup recovery codes generated during initial enrollment.

## Browser Troubleshooting
* If the login button does not respond or reloads the same page, clear your browser cookies and site cache, disable conflicting ad-blocker extensions, or attempt to log in using an Incognito / Private browsing window.

# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Standard Shipping
* **Delivery Window**: Orders are typically delivered within **3 to 5 business days** following order confirmation.
* **Carrier Partners**: We partner with FedEx, UPS, and DHL for domestic and international deliveries.
* **Order Tracking**: Every order includes a tracking number sent via email and SMS within 24 hours of dispatch. Customers can track their packages at any time via the "Track Order" tab on our website or account dashboard.

## Expedited & Express Shipping
* **Next-Day Delivery**: Available for orders placed before 1:00 PM EST Monday through Friday.
* **2-Day Priority**: Guaranteed delivery within 2 business days.

## Delayed or Late Shipments
* **Transit Delays**: Couriers occasionally experience unexpected delays due to severe weather, high holiday volume, or logistical routing issues.
* **24-48 Hour Buffer**: If your scheduled delivery date passed yesterday, please allow a **24 to 48 hour buffer period**, as carriers often update delivery statuses or attempt redelivery the next business day.
* **Lost Package Investigation**: If your package has not moved or arrived after **3 consecutive business days** past the estimated delivery date, contact support to file a carrier claim or request an immediate replacement shipment.

## Modifying Shipping Address
* **Within 1 Hour**: Customers can update the shipping address directly from their account dashboard under "Recent Orders" within **1 hour** of placing the order.
* **After 1 Hour**: Once an order moves into fulfillment and warehouse packaging, the address cannot be changed, and packages must be redirected via the carrier's delivery management portal (e.g., FedEx Delivery Manager).
